## URL action contract

- `restore_200`: same historical intent with original, licensed, or independently reproduced content.
- `redirect_301` or `redirect_308`: only to a substantially intent-equivalent final URL.
- `404`: no useful equivalent and no reason to declare deliberate permanent removal.
- `410`: deliberately retired spam, unsafe, rights-blocked, or permanently removed material.
- `noindex_200`: useful ownership, rights, or due-diligence page that is not launch content.
- `hold`: evidence or rights are unresolved.
- Never redirect unrelated legacy URLs in bulk to the homepage.
- Never copy archived text, code, images, identities, customers, or testimonials without rights.

## Remaining evidence gaps

- Complete live/lost backlink coverage is not proven by a 100-row sample.
- Brand, trademark, former-operator, author, contributor, and archive rights require separate review.
- Search Console manual actions, security issues, current index state, and crawl errors require verified property access.
- Final URL actions require a human or explicitly authorized agent decision recorded in the domain dossier or repository manifest.
